## 🔍 Key Jurisdictional Takeaways

### 1. The Leanest Solo Founder Options
* **Michigan**: Low initial barrier (\$50) and the lowest active annual fee in the continental US (\$25/yr). Total 3-year outlay is only \$125.
* **Wyoming**: The default standard for digital nomads requiring member anonymity, zero state income tax, and full remote fintech banking compatibility.

### 2. The Delaware Cost Disparity
* While Delaware is optimal for venture-backed startups issuing preferred stock as a C-Corporation, choosing Delaware for a pass-through LLC incurs a mandatory \$400/year Franchise Tax, costing **\$1,180+ more than Michigan over 3 years** with zero tax advantages for non-US residents.

### 3. Strict State Late Penalties
* **Florida**: Submitting an annual report after May 1 incurs a strict, non-negotiable **\$400 late fee**.
