# IRS Compliance & Federal Reporting for Foreign Owners

Even if a foreign-owned single-member LLC operates completely outside the United States with 0% US tax liability, federal informational reporting is mandatory under IRS regulations.

---

## 1. Obtaining an EIN Without an SSN or ITIN

Non-US residents cannot use the IRS online portal (which strictly requires a valid SSN or ITIN).

### Standard Direct Procedure:
1. Complete **IRS Form SS-4**.
2. Write "Foreign / None" on Line 7b.
3. Fax the signed application to the IRS international fax unit:
   * **+1 (855) 641-6935** (inside US toll-free)
   * **+1 (304) 707-9471** (international callers)
4. Receive official IRS 147C EIN confirmation letter via return fax (typically 15–20 business days).

---

## 2. The \$25,000 Trap: IRS Form 5472 & Form 1120

A foreign-owned Single-Member LLC is treated as a **Disregarded Entity** for federal income tax purposes, but as a domestic corporation for Section 6038A informational reporting.

### Mandatory Annual Package:
* **Form 1120**: Pro-forma return containing company identification.
* **Form 5472**: Detailed schedule of reportable transactions between the foreign owner and the US entity (including capital contributions, distributions, and loans).

### Critical Rules:
* **Filing Deadline**: April 15 following the tax year.
* **Statutory Penalty**: **\$25,000 per failure to file or untimely filing**, strictly enforced even when gross business revenue is \$0.
